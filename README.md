# Debug & Build (buggy)

Welcome to **Debug & Build**! This repository is designed for developers who have mastered the basics and are ready to test their skills against real-world challenges. Inside, you will find projects that are intentionally broken, contain hidden bugs, or lack critical features. Your task? Fix them, improve them, and showcase your problem-solving skills.

This is the official challenge repository of [LOOM](https://github.com/L-OO-M) — a multi-tenant developer-growth platform for colleges.

## How it works for viewers (external contributors)

Fix bugs through your own fork — you never need write access to this repository:

1. **Fork** this repository to your GitHub account.
2. **Pick a domain** and open the challenge project you want to fix.
3. **Fix the bugs** in your fork. Keep your changes inside that domain's folder.
4. **Commit and push** to your fork with a clear message (what you fixed and why).
5. **Submit your fork** on the LOOM website — paste the URL of your forked repository.
6. **Leads review** your submission and brief you on what you got right and what still needs work.

## How it works for LOOM members

LOOM members work **directly in this repository** on a personal branch and open a pull request. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full guide.
